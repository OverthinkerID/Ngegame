# Changelog — Overthinker-Ngegame

Seluruh perubahan penting pada proyek **Overthinker-Ngegame** dicatat dalam berkas ini.

## [0.1.0] - 2026-11-19

### Ditambahkan
- **Fase 1–12**: Fondasi monorepo TypeScript, Dashboard Desktop, Integrasi Steam lokal, Game Capture & Vision Engine (Mac/Windows), AI Brain Engine (Ollama & OpenAI fallback), Game Controller modular dengan allowlist, Sistem Adaptor Game (7 genre + Universal + Stardew Valley), Human Supervision (L1/L2/L3 + Emergency Stop), AI Voice Studio (**VoxCPM 2.0** Bahasa Indonesia), Integrasi OBS WebSocket v5 & Multistream (Credential Manager), Analitik & Auto-Throttling, serta Backup/Recovery dan konfigurasi `electron-builder.yml`.
- **Redesign UI/UX**: Sistem tema berbasis CSS variables (**Dark Mode** `#0B0D12` & **Light Mode** `#F4F6FA`), komponen dropdown kustom `AppSelect`, `ConfirmDialog`, `PerformanceChart`, `GlobalStatusBar`, logo resmi NGEGAME transparan, dan ikon merek asli Steam/OBS/YouTube/Twitch.
- **Repository & CI**: Penambahan `.github/workflows/ci.yml`, `.github/workflows/release.yml`, serta dokumentasi standar (`CONTRIBUTING.md`, `DEVELOPMENT.md`, `ARCHITECTURE.md`, `RELEASE.md`, `CHANGELOG.md`).
